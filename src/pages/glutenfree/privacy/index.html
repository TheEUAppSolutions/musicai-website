Gluten Free Finder & Scanner Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Music AI Studio Ltd ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use the Gluten Free Finder & Scanner app for iPhone (the "App"). We are the data controller for personal data processed through the App. If you have any questions, email us at info@musicaistudio.app.

In short: the App has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we don't track you. Your diets and the menus you save stay on your iPhone. A menu you photograph is sent to our server to be read and is not kept; what we keep is the menu's text, so the next person who scans the same menu gets it straight away. Finding restaurants near you uses Apple Maps on your iPhone; your location is never sent to us.

1. What stays on your device

The diets you pick (for example gluten-free, lactose-free or vegan) and the menus you scan or open are stored only on your iPhone, so they open again without a connection. Your diets are never sent to us: the App receives every dish's verdict for every diet and applies yours on your iPhone. Deleting the App deletes all of this.

2. Scanning a menu

When you photograph a menu, or choose photos of one, the App sends the photos and your language to our server, run by us on Google Cloud. The server has the menu read, translated and checked against diets by OpenAI's AI models (OpenAI, L.L.C., USA), which process it under their API terms and do not use it to train their models. The photos are used only to read the menu and are not stored. What we keep is the result — the menu's dishes, their translations and verdicts — filed under a fingerprint of the photo, so the same menu isn't read twice. Please photograph only the menu: anything else in the picture is sent too.

3. Finding restaurants and their menus

Restaurants near you, and searches by name, are found by Apple Maps on your iPhone, under Apple's privacy policy. Your location is not sent to us.

When you open a restaurant's menu, the App sends the restaurant's name, address, website and map position, and your language, to our server. The server looks for the menu online (with OpenAI's web search), reads it from the restaurant's own website or a menu site, and reads what the restaurant's own website says about how it keeps gluten out of the food (a dedicated fryer, separate prep, trained staff and so on). We keep the restaurant's menu and those statements, so other people opening the same restaurant get them straight away. To rank the restaurants around you, the App also sends the names and map positions of the restaurants on screen; the answer is how well each known menu fits each diet.

None of these requests includes a name, account or identifier of yours. Like any internet request they carry your device's IP address, which our server uses only to limit how many requests one connection can make, and which our hosting provider keeps in its request logs for a short time.

4. Permissions

The App asks only when a feature needs it:

  • Camera: to photograph a menu, as described in section 2.
  • Photos: the App uses the iOS photo picker, so it only ever receives the photos you choose.
  • Location (while using the App): to find the restaurants around you with Apple Maps, as described in section 3. It is not sent to us.
  • Notifications: to tell you when a menu has finished reading while you were in another app.

5. Analytics, ads and tracking

The App shows no ads and contains no advertising or analytics software. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we don't sell or share your personal data. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with us, under Apple's privacy policy.

6. International transfers

Our server runs on Google Cloud in the United States, and menus are read by OpenAI in the United States. Where data about people in the UK or EU is transferred there, it is protected by safeguards the law recognises, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK's International Data Transfer Addendum.

7. How long we keep it

Menu photos are not kept. The menus we have read, and what restaurants' websites say about their kitchens, are kept as long as they are useful to other people opening them; they are about restaurants, not about you. Request logs are kept by our hosting provider for up to 30 days. What is on your device stays until you delete it or the App.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under UK and EU data protection law and US state privacy laws, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict how it's used, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Because the App keeps your diets and menus on your iPhone, you can delete them there at any time. For anything else, email info@musicaistudio.app. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
