Oil Well Navigator

Find oil and gas wells and get directions to the wellsite.

Oil Well Navigator finds oil and gas wells across Texas, Oklahoma, New Mexico, North Dakota and Wyoming by API number, well name, lease or operator, shows every well around you on a satellite map, and sends the wellsite to Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze. Download a state to search and see the map with no signal.

Contact us

Email info@musicaistudio.app. Tell us the well's API number if a well is missing or in the wrong place, and we'll look into it.

Questions

Where does the well data come from?

From the public records of each state's oil and gas regulator, refreshed every night. Locations can be off by some distance, and Texas doesn't publish operators in its map data. Always follow the lease signs and the operator's instructions on site.

How do I use it with no signal?

Open the Offline tab and download your state on Wi-Fi. Search and the map then work without a connection. Offline packs are part of Pro.

How do I restore or cancel Pro?

To restore, open the paywall (Settings › Get Pro) and tap Restore. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your iPhone's Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple.
