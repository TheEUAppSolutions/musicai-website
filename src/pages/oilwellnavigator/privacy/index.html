Oil Well Navigator Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Music AI Studio Ltd ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use the Oil Well Navigator app for iPhone (the "App"). We are the data controller for personal data processed through the App. If you have any questions, email us at info@musicaistudio.app.

In short: the App has no accounts and no ads, and we don't track you or sell your data. Your location stays on your iPhone. To show and find wells, the App sends our server what you search for and the area of the map you are looking at, with no name, account or identifier of yours. Purchases are made with Apple; our subscription service records them under an anonymous ID so Pro works on your devices.

1. Finding and showing wells

When you search (an API number, a well or lease name, or an operator), the App sends the search to our server, run by us on Google Cloud. When you look at the map, it sends the edges of the area on screen, so the server can answer with the wells inside it. When you open a well, it sends that well's ID. If you have downloaded the state, the App answers from your iPhone instead and sends nothing.

None of these requests includes a name, account or identifier of yours. Like any internet request they carry your device's IP address, which our hosting provider keeps in its request logs for a short time. We don't use the searches or map areas to identify you or build a profile of you.

2. Your location

If you allow it ("while using the App"), the App shows where you are on the map with Apple's MapKit, under Apple's privacy policy. Your location itself is not sent to us. The map area you are looking at is (section 1), and it is often around where you are.

3. Offline packs and what stays on your device

An offline pack is a copy of a state's public well records, downloaded from our server and kept on your iPhone so search and the map work without signal. The App also keeps on your iPhone whether you've seen the introduction and how many free directions you've used. Deleting the App deletes all of this.

4. Directions in other maps apps

When you ask for directions, the App opens Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze with the well's coordinates. From then on that app's own privacy policy applies.

5. Purchases and Pro

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are sold and billed by Apple; we never see your payment details. To know whether you have Pro, and to restore it on another device, the App uses our subscription service, Horse Revenue (run by us on Google Cloud). It receives the App Store transactions for your purchases, an anonymous ID created by the App, your device's region and the App's version, and when a paywall is shown, closed or used to buy. If you came from an Apple Search Ads advert, the App sends Apple's attribution token once, so we can tell which adverts work; Apple answers with the campaign, not with who you are. This does not need, and the App does not ask for, permission to track you.

6. Analytics, ads and tracking

The App shows no ads and contains no third-party advertising or analytics software. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we don't sell or share your personal data. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with us, under Apple's privacy policy.

7. Where the well data comes from

The wells are public records published by the oil and gas regulators of Texas, Oklahoma, New Mexico, North Dakota and Wyoming. They describe wells and their operators, not you.

8. International transfers

Our servers run on Google Cloud in the United States. Where data about people in the UK or EU is transferred there, it is protected by safeguards the law recognises, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK's International Data Transfer Addendum.

9. How long we keep it

Searches and map areas are answered and not stored, apart from the request logs, which our hosting provider keeps for up to 30 days. Purchase records are kept while you have Pro and as long as tax and accounting law requires. What is on your device stays until you delete it or the App.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under UK and EU data protection law and US state privacy laws, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict how it's used, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Email info@musicaistudio.app. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

11. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
