Help

Put FrameCast on your Roku

A couple of minutes with the remote. Then your photos come on by themselves whenever the TV rests.

Find FrameCast on your TV

On the TV, press Home, then go to Settings › Theme › Screensavers and pick FrameCast.

FrameCast is on its way to the Roku store. Once it's there, it shows up in that list, and you can also add it from the Framecast TV app on your iPhone or at channelstore.roku.com, then go straight to step 3.

Get it

Choose Get screensaver. It's free, and the TV installs it in a few seconds.

Make it your screensaver

Choose Set as screensaver. From now on, your photos come on by themselves whenever the TV rests.

To change how long the TV waits first, go back to Theme and choose Screensaver wait time. 5 minutes is a good start.

Link your TV to the app

Coming soon: you'll pick your TV in the Framecast TV app on your iPhone, and your albums go straight to it.

If you get stuck

I added it, but it isn't on the Home screen.

That's normal. A screensaver isn't an app you open: it lives in Settings › Theme › Screensavers, and once it's chosen there it starts by itself when nobody has touched the remote for the wait time.

I don't have the remote.

The free Roku app for iPhone and Android works as a remote when your phone is on the same Wi-Fi as the TV.

I'm setting it up for someone else.

Steps 1 to 3 are done on their TV with its remote, so send them this page or do it together on a visit. After that, you choose the photos from your own phone.
