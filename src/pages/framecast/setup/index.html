Put FrameCast on your Roku

Three steps, a couple of minutes. Then your photos come on by themselves whenever the TV rests.

Add FrameCast to your Roku

FrameCast is on its way to the Roku store. Once it's there, add it from the Framecast TV app on your iPhone, or at channelstore.roku.com: sign in with the Roku account your TV uses and choose Add. The TV downloads it by itself, no remote needed.

Or on the TV: press Home, open Streaming Store and search for FrameCast.

Make it your screensaver

On the TV, press Home, then go to Settings › Theme › Screensavers and choose FrameCast.

In Change wait time, pick how long the TV waits before your photos come on. 5 minutes is a good start.

Link your TV to the app

Coming soon: you'll pick your TV in the Framecast TV app on your iPhone, and your albums go straight to it.

If you get stuck

I added it, but it isn't on the Home screen.

That's normal. A screensaver isn't an app you open: it lives in Settings › Theme › Screensavers, and once it's chosen there it starts by itself when nobody has touched the remote for the wait time.

I don't have the remote.

The free Roku app for iPhone and Android works as a remote when your phone is on the same Wi-Fi as the TV.

I'm setting it up for someone else.

Step 1 needs the Roku account their TV uses, and step 2 is done on their TV, so send them this page or do it together on a visit. After that, you choose the photos from your own phone.
