Framecast TV Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Music AI Studio Ltd ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use the Framecast TV app for iPhone and iPad (the "App"). We are the data controller for personal data processed through the App. If you have any questions, email us at info@musicaistudio.app.

In short: the App has no accounts and no ads, and we don't track you or sell your data. The photos you pick and the albums you make stay on your iPhone or iPad; we never receive them. Purchases are made with Apple; our subscription service records them, and how the App is used, under an anonymous ID.

1. Your photos and albums

You choose photos with Apple's photo picker, which gives the App only the photos you select, not your library, and doesn't need permission to read your library. The App saves a copy of each photo you choose, resized for a TV screen, in its own storage on your device, as part of an album. When it makes that copy it leaves out the photo's metadata, such as where it was taken. Your photos and albums are not sent to us or to anyone else.

2. Showing photos on your TV

This version of the App keeps your albums on your device. Sending an album to a TV needs our servers to pass the photos on, and that feature is not yet available. Before it is, we will update this policy to say exactly what is stored, where, and for how long, and the App will ask you before any photo leaves your device.

3. Ready-made collections

The ready-made collections are pictures that come with the App. Adding one copies its pictures into an album on your device; nothing about it is sent to us.

4. What else stays on your device

The App also keeps on your device your screensaver choices (which widgets to show, how long each photo stays, whether sound effects play), whether you've finished the introduction, and whether it has asked you for a review. Deleting the App deletes all of this, your albums included.

5. Purchases, Pro and how the App is used

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are sold and billed by Apple; we never see your payment details. To know whether you have Pro, and to restore it on another device, the App uses our subscription service, Horse Revenue (run by us on Google Cloud). It receives the App Store transactions for your purchases, an anonymous ID created by the App, your device's region, language and model, and the App's version.

Under the same anonymous ID, the App reports how it is used, so we can see what works and fix what doesn't: when it is opened and for how long, which screens are shown, which steps of the introduction are completed (and how many photos were picked, as a number), and when a paywall is shown, closed or used to buy. These reports contain no photos, names or contact details. If you came from an Apple Search Ads advert, the App sends Apple's attribution token once, so we can tell which adverts work; Apple answers with the campaign, not with who you are. None of this needs, and the App does not ask for, permission to track you.

6. Ads and tracking

The App shows no ads and contains no third-party advertising or analytics software. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we don't sell or share your personal data. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with us, under Apple's privacy policy.

7. When you email us

"Contact us" and "Suggest a feature" open a new email in your mail app, with the App's version and your iOS version filled in below your message. Nothing is sent unless you send the email. We use what you write, and your email address, only to answer you.

8. International transfers

Horse Revenue runs on Google Cloud in the United States. Where data about people in the UK or EU is transferred there, it is protected by safeguards the law recognises, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK's International Data Transfer Addendum.

9. How long we keep it

We hold no photos or albums. Purchase records are kept while you have Pro and as long as tax and accounting law requires; usage reports are kept for up to two years. Emails are kept while we're helping you and for a reasonable time after. What is on your device stays until you delete it or the App.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under UK and EU data protection law and US state privacy laws, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict how it's used, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Email info@musicaistudio.app. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

11. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, and will before the App can send photos to a TV. We will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
