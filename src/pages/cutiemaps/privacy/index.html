Cutie Maps Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Music AI Studio Ltd ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use the Cutie Maps app for iPhone and CarPlay (the "App"). We are the data controller for personal data processed through the App. If you have any questions, email us at info@musicaistudio.app.

In short: the App has no accounts and no ads, and we don't track you. Your location is used to show the map, find places and guide you, and is sent to the map and weather services that do that work, never to our own servers. What you save and design in the App stays on your iPhone.

1. What stays on your device

Your map theme, navigation voice and other settings, your saved and recent places, the CarPlay widgets you design and the photos you add to them are stored only on your iPhone, in storage shared with the App's widgets so they can show in CarPlay. None of this is sent to us. Deleting the App deletes it.

The navigation voices are recordings that come with the App, and spoken directions are made on your iPhone. The App doesn't use your microphone.

2. Your location

With your permission, the App uses your device's location while you're using it: to show where you are on the map, find places near you, plan routes and give turn-by-turn directions, and to show the weather on your widgets. While you're following directions, guidance carries on when the App is in the background or your screen is locked, and iOS shows that the App is using your location. The App doesn't use your location when you aren't using it or driving with it.

Your location, what you search for and the places you get directions to are sent to the services in section 3, which need them to answer. We don't receive or store your location or your searches on our own servers.

3. Services the App uses

  • Mapbox (Mapbox, Inc., USA) provides the map, place search, routes and navigation. It receives your location, your searches and your destinations to provide them. Mapbox's software in the App also collects telemetry: location, usage and performance data tied to a random identifier that isn't linked to your identity. Mapbox uses it to run and improve its maps and traffic data. You can turn this off at any time from the ⓘ button in the corner of the map (Mapbox Telemetry). See Mapbox's privacy policy.
  • Apple Weather (WeatherKit) provides the weather on your widgets. It receives the location the weather is for. See Apple's privacy policy.
  • Apple processes purchases (section 4) and runs CarPlay, which shows the App on your car's screen.

These providers handle the data under their own privacy policies.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions and other in-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment card or Apple ID details. So the App knows what you've unlocked, our subscription service (run by us on Google Cloud) receives a random ID created for the App when it is installed, the purchase records Apple sends for that ID (product, price, currency, country, dates, trial and renewal status), and your device model, iOS version, App version and language. This isn't linked to your name, email or any other identity.

5. Permissions

The App asks only when a feature needs it:

  • Location (While Using the App): for the map, nearby places, directions and weather, as described in section 2.
  • Photos: the App uses the iOS photo picker, so it only ever receives the pictures you choose for a widget. They're stored on your device, never sent to us.

6. Analytics, ads and tracking

The App shows no ads and contains no advertising or analytics software of our own. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we don't sell or share your personal data for advertising. The only usage data collected is Mapbox's telemetry (section 3), which you can turn off.

7. International transfers

Mapbox and our subscription service process data in the United States. Where data about people in the UK or EU is transferred there, it is protected by safeguards the law recognises, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK's International Data Transfer Addendum.

8. How long we keep it

What is on your device stays until you delete it or the App. Purchase records are kept while your subscription is active and afterwards for as long as we need them to handle refunds, disputes and our legal and tax obligations. Mapbox and Apple keep data under their own policies.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under UK and EU data protection law and US state privacy laws, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict how it's used, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. To use these rights, email info@musicaistudio.app. Because the App has no accounts, tell us the purchase you mean (a receipt from Apple helps) so we can find its records. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
