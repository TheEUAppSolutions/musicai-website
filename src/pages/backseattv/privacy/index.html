Backseat TV Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Music AI Studio Ltd ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use the Backseat TV app for iPhone and iPad (the "App").

In short: we don't collect any personal data. Backseat TV has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. What you add to the App stays on your device.

1. What stays on your device

The playlists, stream links and media server addresses you add, your favourites, where you stopped watching, your settings and the videos you import are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. Sign-in details for your Jellyfin or Emby server are kept in the device's Keychain. None of this is sent to us. Deleting the App deletes it.

2. Connections the App makes

  • What you add. To play something, the App connects directly to the playlist, stream or media server addresses you give it. Those services see your IP address and the requests the App makes, as with any connection, and handle them under their own privacy policies. We don't see these connections.
  • The sample playlist. "Try a sample playlist" downloads a list and short clips from musicaistudio.app, and two free films from the Blender Foundation's servers. Like any website, these servers may log your IP address and the time of the request; we don't combine those logs with anything else or use them to identify you.

3. Permissions

The App asks only when a feature needs it, and everything it reads stays on your device:

  • Motion & Fitness: to tell whether the car is moving, so video sent to a car's display pauses while it moves. It isn't stored or sent anywhere.
  • Camera: to scan a playlist's QR code. Images aren't saved or sent.
  • Photos and Files: only the videos you pick, to play or import them.
  • Local Network: to reach media servers and streams on your own network.

4. Screen mirroring and AirPlay

When you mirror your screen, the picture is processed on your iPhone and sent over your local network to the display you choose with AirPlay. It never passes through our servers.

5. Analytics, ads and tracking

The App contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

6. Children

The App doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children. Parents decide what content is added to it.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
