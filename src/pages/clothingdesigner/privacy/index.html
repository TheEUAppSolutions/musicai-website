Clothing Designer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026 (AI brand studio added)

This Privacy Policy explains how Music AI Studio Ltd ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use the Clothing Designer app for iPhone (the "App"). We are the data controller for personal data processed through the App. If you have any questions, email us at info@musicaistudio.app.

In short: the App has no accounts and no ads, and we don't track you or sell your data. Your designs and the photos you add stay on your iPhone; we never receive them. If you use the AI brand studio, the answers you give it are sent to our server and to OpenAI to make your brand kit. Purchases are made with Apple; our subscription service records them under an anonymous ID so Pro works on your devices.

1. Your designs and photos

Designs are saved on your iPhone, in the App's own storage. When you add a photo, you pick it with Apple's photo picker, which gives the App only the photo you chose, not your library; the App keeps a copy inside the design. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else, and the App doesn't need an account or an internet connection to design.

2. Exports and sharing

When you export a design, the App makes an image on your iPhone and opens Apple's share sheet. If you save it to Photos, the App asks for permission to add images to your library; it can't read your library with that permission. Where the image goes after that (Messages, a print shop, social media) is up to you, under that service's own privacy policy.

3. The AI brand studio

The brand studio on the Create tab is optional. If you use it, the answers you give (your brand's name, where you are based, what you want to make, style, audience, age range, colours, price point, production country, number of pieces and your own description) are sent to our server, run by us on Google Cloud, which passes them to OpenAI to write three design directions and production cost estimates, and to make lookbook photos. Your logo, designs and photos are not sent; the App only says whether you have a logo. Requests carry an anonymous app token and no name, account or identifier of yours. OpenAI processes them under its API terms, which do not allow it to train its models on them; it may keep them for up to 30 days to detect abuse. The brand kit that comes back is saved on your iPhone.

4. What else stays on your device

The App also keeps on your iPhone whether you've seen the introduction, whether it has asked you for a review in this version, and how many clean exports you've used. Deleting the App deletes all of this, your designs included.

5. Purchases and Pro

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are sold and billed by Apple; we never see your payment details. To know whether you have Pro, and to restore it on another device, the App uses our subscription service, Horse Revenue (run by us on Google Cloud). It receives the App Store transactions for your purchases, an anonymous ID created by the App, your device's region and the App's version, and when a paywall is shown, closed or used to buy. If you came from an Apple Search Ads advert, the App sends Apple's attribution token once, so we can tell which adverts work; Apple answers with the campaign, not with who you are. This does not need, and the App does not ask for, permission to track you.

6. Analytics, ads and tracking

The App shows no ads and contains no third-party advertising or analytics software. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we don't sell or share your personal data. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with us, under Apple's privacy policy.

7. International transfers

Horse Revenue runs on Google Cloud in the United States. Where data about people in the UK or EU is transferred there, it is protected by safeguards the law recognises, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK's International Data Transfer Addendum.

8. How long we keep it

We hold no designs or photos. Brand studio answers are passed through our server and not stored by us; OpenAI may keep them for up to 30 days, as above. Purchase records are kept while you have Pro and as long as tax and accounting law requires. What is on your device stays until you delete it or the App.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under UK and EU data protection law and US state privacy laws, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict how it's used, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Email info@musicaistudio.app. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
