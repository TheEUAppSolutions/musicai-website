FAQ

Good questions.

What does Stem Split do?

It uses AI to separate a song into its parts: vocals, drums, bass, guitar, piano and everything else. You can then mute or solo any part, remove the vocals for karaoke, see the chords and lyrics, change the tempo and key, record yourself over the band, and export the stems.

Is it free?

Stem Split is free to download and try. Full access is part of Stem Split Pro, which you can get inside the app as a subscription or a one-time purchase.

Which songs can I use?

Any audio you own or have the rights to use: files (MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, ALAC, FLAC, OGG, MP4), your iTunes library, the sound from videos in your camera roll, a direct link to an audio file, or a recording made in the app. Songs from streaming services like Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube can't be imported.

Can I use the stems in my DAW?

Yes. Export each stem on its own, or choose DAW export for aligned WAV stems with tempo and chords, plus session files that open in Reaper, Cubase, Studio One and Bitwig, and a MIDI tempo map for Pro Tools, Logic and Ableton.

What happens to my audio?

Songs you split are uploaded over an encrypted connection to our servers, which separate them and send the stems back to you. We use your audio only to provide the service and never sell it. The details are in our Privacy Policy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

If you subscribed in the app, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad and choose Stem Split. If you subscribed on our website, use Manage web subscription.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 16.4 or later.
